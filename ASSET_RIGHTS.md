This release includes the complete app and the matching source/resources at the
project owner's request. No online publication is performed by the packaging tool.

code-drawn companion emblem are covered by the application MIT license.
The included Anno artwork, game terms, dialogue and extracted catalogue originate
from Anno 117: Pax Romana and remain Ubisoft materials. The MIT license does not
grant rights in those materials. This app is unofficial and is not endorsed by
Ubisoft. Third-party libraries retain their own licenses (see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES).

Provenance: the local extracted patch 2.0 production, consumption, specialist,
icon, region-art and official-language text datasets. The app keeps nominal
catalogue data separate from experimental pipe observations.

References reviewed:

redistributing the included game materials. The package makes no such claim.

No private capture, pairing secret, device token, Windows password, local SDK path
or device settings are included. Demo islands and statistics are synthetic.

## Distribution scope selected by the project owner

On 2026-11-01, the project owner explicitly reaffirmed a complete free application
with the game resources actually used by its interface, catalogue and quotes,
and optional coffee support, citing established Anno community tools and fan-tool
promotion. That is the intended packaging scope. Preserve the ownership and
unofficial-status notices above; do not represent this decision as a separately
obtained Ubisoft authorization. Raw game archives and unused full extraction dumps
are outside the package. Publication remains a separate user-authorized action.
